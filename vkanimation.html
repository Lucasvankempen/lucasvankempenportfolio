VK Animation

Over time, I expanded my work to include creating custom thumbnails for YouTubers, helping them enhance their online presence and engagement.

Later on, I taught myself to create 3D animations using Blender and After Effects. I really enjoyed the creative process and also had the opportunity to work on several client projects. Below, you can view my showreel featuring some of my best work.

When I started at Fontys ICT, I stopped doing client work. I want to focus on learning new things, and once I have done that, I will return to some form of entrepreneurship.
